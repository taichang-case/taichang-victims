English

What this is

An independent researcher is compiling a public-source case file about an investment-fraud operation that used the following names and domains. If you deposited money through any of them, your report may help investigators.

Name / domainStatus
tickmilleas.comSeized by the FBI (seizure banner)
fortuneprimeglobalirts.comSeized by the FBI
"Fortune Prime Global" (impersonation)Brand impersonation, multiple domains
"TICKMILL" (impersonation)Brand impersonation
"JHBHOLY" mobile appAdvance-fee / "withdrawal tax" scheme

If you used a different domain or app name that looks similar, it may still be the same operation. Report it anyway and describe what you saw.

How to report

  • United States: FBI IC3 — ic3.gov; FBI tip line for this case — TaiChangTIPS@fbi.gov; FBI San Diego — (858) 320-1800; tips.fbi.gov
  • United Kingdom: Action Fraud — actionfraud.police.uk
  • Australia: Scamwatch — scamwatch.gov.au
  • Canada: Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre — antifraudcentre-centreantifraude.ca
  • Elsewhere: your national police cybercrime unit

What to include in your report

  • Dates and times of each payment (with timezone)
  • Amounts and currencies, and the payment method used
  • The wallet address or bank account you sent funds to, and transaction IDs if available
  • Screenshots of the platform, chat messages, and any "withdrawal tax" or "verification fee" demands
  • Names, handles or phone numbers used by the "account manager" or "customer service"

What NOT to send

  • Full ID documents, passwords, 2FA codes, or bank login details
  • Any further payment — no legitimate recovery process requires an upfront fee
Contact for the case file (optional): if you want your public statement included in the file submitted to investigators, write to taichang-case@outlook.com. Do not send ID documents. You can also report anonymously through IC3.
